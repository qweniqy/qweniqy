A small first step

A careful first look.
Nothing more than you need.

A free, brief review of one redacted screenshot or pasted message. Limited daily places keep it manageable; when the pilot is open, a daily cap and response window are shown here.

01 / SHARE

Send one message, safely

Share one redacted screenshot or paste the text. Remove passwords, one-time codes, bank details and unrelated personal information first.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Get a limited first pass

We point out visible clues and explain what they may mean. If evidence or context is thin, we will say so plainly. A screenshot alone cannot prove who sent something.

03 / DECIDE

Choose a sensible next step

If more context would help, an optional 60-minute consultation offers a careful assessment and practical next steps. Its fixed price is shown before booking.
